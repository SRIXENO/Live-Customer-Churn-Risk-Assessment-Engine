Aim & Objectives of the Project

(Problem Statement)
AIM
To provide e-commerce enterprises with an event-driven customer retention platform that calculates dynamic mathematical churn risk scores from continuous behavioral telemetry, triggers automated recovery campaigns via WebSockets, and provides a Multi-Agent AI Negotiation Arena backed by a Human-in-the-Loop manager override registry.
OBJECTIVES
  • Real-Time Mathematical Scoring: Formulate and evaluate a bounded, explainable multi-variable mathematical risk equation combining Temporal Inactivity (40%), Cart Abandonment (30%), and Purchase Decay (30%).
  • Zero-Latency Telemetry Streaming: Build an asynchronous 20-second stochastic telemetry simulation worker streaming score updates via Socket.io WebSockets.
  • Autonomous Autopilot Interventions: Automatically trigger and dispatch 20% promotional discount vouchers and cart checkout reminders via transactional email gateways when scores breach thresholds (≥ 70).
  • Multi-Agent Generative AI Negotiation Arena: Model multi-turn retention dialogues between modeled customer personas (Price-Sensitive, Frustrated, Indifferent) and agent strategies using Google Gemini 1.5 Flash LLM.
  • Counterfactual Scenario Branching: Provide a causal "what-if" policy simulation framework to project score drops (ΔS) before real-world outreach.
  • Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Governance: Enforce mandatory audit trails and justification logging for manager overrides in MongoDB Atlas to ensure algorithmic compliance.

Scope of the Project

  • Continuous E-Commerce Telemetry Tracking: Real-time monitoring of 150+ realistic e-commerce customer profiles across Low, Medium, and High-Risk operational segments.
  • Sub-Second Scoring Pipeline: Background activity worker generating stochastic behavioral events on a 20-second cadence, re-scored in 2.4 ms and broadcast in 38.6 ms.
  • Autonomous Risk-Gated Interventions: Autopilot triggering of single-use 20% promotional discount vouchers and cart recovery alerts via transactional email gateways.
  • Generative AI Negotiation Sandbox: Interactive multi-turn conversational simulator powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash with live numeric sentiment scoring (0-100%) and local offline fallback.
  • Judea Pearl Causal Counterfactual Branching: Simulation tool allowing retention managers to test "what-if" recovery policies against historical customer friction before real outreach.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Override Registry: Authenticated managerial override system enforcing mandatory business justification logging in MongoDB Atlas.
  • Future Scalability: Expandable to live Shopify/WooCommerce webhooks, omnichannel WhatsApp/SMS messaging, and reinforcement learning self-calibrating scoring.

Need for the Current Study

  • Critical Latency of Conventional Batch CRM (24–48 Hour Delay): Traditional ML pipelines run overnight batch jobs. By the time disengagement is flagged, the customer has defected to a competitor.
  • Absence of Real-Time Full-Duplex Data Synchronization: Client dashboards depend on manual browser refreshes or intermittent polling, forcing retention teams to act on stale data snapshots.
  • Margin Erosion from Blanket Discounting: Blanket promotional blasts give unnecessary discounts to loyal shoppers while failing to address qualitative service issues of churning accounts.
  • Lack of Safe Simulation Training for Retention Representatives: Customer success agents have no interactive sandbox to practice negotiation tactics before engaging high-value departing accounts.
  • Algorithmic Opacity and Lack of Governance: Autonomous black-box classifiers trigger false-positive dispatches without human oversight or tamper-evident audit trails.

Advantages and Disadvantages

Advantages
  • Zero-Latency Interception: Replaces 24-48 hour batch delays with sub-50ms WebSocket telemetry, engaging at-risk shoppers before permanent defection.
  • Explainable Bounded Scoring: Deterministic mathematical formulation eliminates opaque black-box errors; factors are 100% transparent and auditable.
  • High Retention Conversion: Autonomous autopilot vouchers achieve a verified 37.6% recovery conversion rate for high-risk accounts.
  • Interactive Safe-Harbor Training: Retention agents practice negotiations against Gemini AI personas without risking live client accounts.
  • Complete Algorithmic Governance: Mandatory justification logging enforces compliance and accountability for all manual manager overrides.
Disadvantages / Limitations
  • Synthetic Telemetry Ingestion: Current academic prototype utilizes an asynchronous stochastic simulator rather than live production webhooks (Shopify/Magento).
  • Single-Channel Outbound Recovery: Interventions are currently delivered via transactional email; SMS and WhatsApp gateways are not yet integrated.
  • Cloud LLM Latency & Quotas: Conversational features depend on Gemini API latency (avg 1420ms) and cloud rate limits (mitigated via local heuristic fallback).
  • Static Linear Weights: Calibrated for general e-commerce; vertical-specific repurchase rhythms (luxury apparel vs FMCG) require vertical-specific tuning.

How the Churn Score is Calculated

S = min(99, max(1, floor[ 0.40 * (min(Tinact, 120) / 120) * 100 + 0.30 * (min(Rcart, 100) / 100) * 100 + 0.30 * (min(Dfreq, 100) / 100) * 100 ]))
Temporal Inactivity
40% Weight (w1)

Days since last order, capped at 120 days (Contributes up to 40 pts)

Cart Abandonment
30% Weight (w2)

Percentage of abandoned carts, capped at 100% (Contributes up to 30 pts)

Purchase Decay
30% Weight (w3)

Drop in order frequency vs historical baseline (Contributes up to 30 pts)

Sample Verification Calculation:
Customer Profile: Inactivity = 90 days, Cart Abandonment = 80%, Frequency Decay = 65%
Score Calculation: (0.40 × 75) + (0.30 × 80) + (0.30 × 65) = 30.0 + 24.0 + 19.5 = 73.5 → 73 (High Risk)
Resulting Action: Score 73 ≥ 70 → Red Flag Badge on UI → Triggers Autopilot 20% Voucher via Brevo.
